A positive value is a hard cap: the engine compresses the overflow column — font-size, vertical spacing, inter-card gap and intra-card horizontal whitespace — until the deck fits. The density factor is near-unbounded (floor 0.15), so readability is sacrificed when needed to honor the cap.
